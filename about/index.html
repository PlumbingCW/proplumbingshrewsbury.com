Who We Are

Shrewsbury Pro Plumbing is a plumbing service for Shrewsbury, Massachusetts. We take emergency calls around the clock and book planned work for homes and businesses in Shrewsbury and the nearby towns of Westborough, Grafton, Northborough, Boylston and east Worcester.

We keep the message simple. A plumber comes to the address, finds the cause, explains the options and gives a clear price before work starts.

How a Call Works

You call the 24/7 line and a dispatcher asks for your address and what is happening. If water is flowing, they talk you through shutting it off while a plumber is lined up. Planned work is booked with same-day appointments when available.

We do not promise an arrival time we cannot control, and we do not quote a price over the phone for work nobody has seen. The visit comes first.

What We Cover

Drains and sewers, water heaters and tankless units, leaks and burst pipes, boilers, sump pumps, basement waterproofing, fixtures, disposals and commercial plumbing. The services page lists every one with its own guide.

Older homes get extra attention. Shrewsbury grew as a streetcar suburb and its population roughly doubled between 1915 and 1940, so cast iron drains, galvanized supply and original basement fixtures are part of daily work.

How We Work

Each job begins with a diagnosis. We show you what we found, explain what is urgent and what can wait, and let you decide. Repair and replacement are both on the table, and we say which one we would choose and why.

We keep the work area clean, test what we touched and tell you what to watch for afterward. If we cannot do a job well, we say so.

What You Will Not Get From Us

You will not get a pressure pitch, a made-up deadline or a price that changes once the work starts. You will not get a promise of when we will arrive, because traffic, weather and the job before yours are real.

You will get a plain explanation, a written scope and a plumber who tells you what can wait. That is the whole approach, and it is the reason the guides on this site are written the way they are.

Why We Publish So Many Guides

A homeowner who understands the problem makes a better decision, and a better decision is better for everyone. Each guide on the blog explains a task or a choice in plain language, including the parts that are not exciting, like when a do-it-yourself fix goes wrong.

We would rather you read the guide and fix a clogged toilet yourself than pay for a visit you did not need. When the guide says to call a plumber, it is because the risk of doing it alone is real.

How We Decide What to Recommend

We start with the cheapest fix that will last and move up only when the evidence says to. A water heater with a leaking tank gets replaced, while one with a bad thermostat gets repaired. A drain with a single clog gets cleared, while one with roots in every joint gets a camera and a plan.

Whatever we recommend, you hear the reason in plain language, and you see the clear price before work starts.

Where to Start on This Site

If water is moving right now, call the line. If you are comparing options, the services page lists every job with its own guide, the areas page shows the towns we reach and the blog holds our longer how-to guides.

If you are still deciding whether a problem is urgent, read the preparation checklist on the matching service page and call if any item on it describes what you see.

Who We Serve

Homeowners, landlords, property managers and small businesses in Shrewsbury and nearby towns. A restaurant with a grease line, a landlord with a shared drain stack and a family with a failed water heater all get the same careful diagnosis.

If your building is older, tell us when you call. Age changes what we check first.

Our Standards

We do not use scare tactics. We explain the likely cause, the options and the price before anything starts.

Each blog post ends with a section on Shrewsbury conditions, and each service page has a preparation checklist you can follow before we arrive.
