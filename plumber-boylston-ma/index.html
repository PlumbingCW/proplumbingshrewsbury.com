Wells, Septic and Town Water: Know Which You Have

Boylston is mostly residential, with about 4,800 residents in the 2020 census. The Boylston Water District supplies roughly 3,300 people according to federal records, which suggests a meaningful share of homes run on private wells instead. A well house has a pump and pressure tank, and a town-water house has a service line and meter.

That difference decides who we call and what we check. Tell the dispatcher which you have, because a pressure complaint on a well can come from the pump, not the pipes.

Storm Season and Snowmelt: When Basements Test the Pump

Spring snowmelt and summer thunderstorms are when basements take on water. A sump pump that has sat idle all winter may be stuck or unpowered when the first heavy rain arrives. Pour a bucket of water into the pit to test it, and if it fails, call before the storm, not during it.

A water-level alarm or a battery backup is a sensible addition for a house that sits low or has a history of damp walls. Our sump pump repair page covers the pump types and what a visit involves.
