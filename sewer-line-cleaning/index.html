Sewer Backup: What to Do First

If sewage is rising in a tub, shower or floor drain, stop using water in the whole house, including the washer and dishwasher. Keep children and pets out of the area and do not flush. Call the 24/7 line right away, because every gallon you add goes into the same blocked pipe.

Why Main Sewer Lines Block

The main sewer line carries everything from the house to the town connection or septic system. Roots are the most common reason it blocks, since tiny hairs enter at a joint and grow into a mat. Grease, wipes and paper catch on that mat and form a plug.

Older houses make this worse. A line built from clay tile or old cast iron has many joints, and each one is a place for roots to get in. Shrewsbury has many such houses from its early and mid-1900s growth, so root problems are a regular part of local plumbing.

Signs the Main Line Is the Problem

Dispatch is faster when you can describe what the house is doing.

  • Several fixtures back up together: a toilet, a tub and a floor drain in one backup point to the main, not a branch that drain cleaning could reach.
  • The lowest drain fails first: a basement floor drain or shower overflows before the upstairs fixtures show a problem.
  • Toilet bubbles when the washer drains: air is being pushed past a partial blockage.
  • Slow drains all over the house: a gradual buildup in the main shows up everywhere at once.
  • Wet spot or sunken patch in the yard: a collapsed or broken line can leak waste into the soil.

What Happens During a Sewer Line Cleaning

A good cleaning has four stages.

  1. 1Find the access point.The plumber locates the cleanout, or pulls a toilet if none exists, and sets up away from the wet area.
  2. 2Cut through the blockage.A heavy cable machine with a root cutter opens the line, or a jetter clears it if grease is the cause.
  3. 3Flush and test.Water runs through the line to confirm it flows freely, and the plumber measures how far the cable went.
  4. 4Look inside.A sewer camera inspection shows the roots, cracks or offsets that caused the blockage, so you know if cleaning is enough.

If the camera finds a cracked pipe, the options are laid out under sewer line repair, and a scaled or grease-coated line may call for hydro jetting.

Town Sewer, Septic and Who Owns What

Most Shrewsbury homeowners are responsible for the sewer pipe from the house to the point where it meets the town main. If you are not sure whether your street has public sewer, or where your responsibility ends, ask the town's public works office before work starts.

A house on septic needs a different check. A backup on a septic system can come from a full tank or a failed leach field, not a blocked pipe, and clearing the line will not fix that. Septic systems are more common in smaller neighboring towns such as Boylston than on town-sewered Shrewsbury streets.
