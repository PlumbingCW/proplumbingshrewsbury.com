The Calls That Come From Northborough Homes

A house with several bathrooms and a finished basement puts steady demand on the water heater, the drains and the sump pump. When any of those fails, the effect reaches more of the house. Water heater leaks, hidden supply leaks and drain backups are the problems we plan for most in Northborough.

Tell the dispatcher what you can see, and we will line up the right visit. A tank leaking onto a finished floor is a water heater replacement call, while a spreading ceiling stain needs leak detection before anyone cuts drywall.

Reservoir Water and What It Means for Your Fixtures

Northborough's public water comes through the MWRA system from the Quabbin and Wachusett reservoirs, delivered through roughly 65 miles of town main. The town maintains the main and the hydrants, and your responsibility starts at the meter. That line from the meter to your house is where many hidden leaks begin, and water service line repair covers it.

Because the supply is treated surface water and not a local well, a plumber's first questions about a fixture problem tend to be about age and installation, not water chemistry.
