Privacy Policy

Updated November 12, 2026

What we collect when you call or visit, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Call (508) 389-4045

Overview

This policy explains what information Shrewsbury Pro Plumbing collects when you visit proplumbingshrewsbury.com or call (508) 389-4045, and how we use it. The site has no forms, no accounts and no payment pages.

We keep this short because there is not much to collect.

Information We Collect

When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address and a description of the problem so we can send a plumber. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

When you visit the site, our hosting provider and standard web tools may record technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. We use that data to keep the site working and to understand how it is used.

How We Use It

We use call information to dispatch a plumber, give a price, follow up on the visit and improve our service. We use site data to maintain and improve the website.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with people who help us do the work, such as a plumber assigned to your address, or when the law requires it. Service providers that host the site or handle calls follow their own privacy terms.

Cookies

The site may use basic cookies or similar tools for performance. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

Your Choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to legal and business record requirements. Email contact@proplumbingshrewsbury.com or call the number above.

Children

The site is intended for adults who own or manage property. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the bottom of the page shows the latest revision.
