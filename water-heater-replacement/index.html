Failed Tank: What to Do Before We Arrive

If the tank is leaking heavily, shut the cold supply valve on top of the heater, switch off its power or gas and use a wet vac or mop to protect flooring. Photograph the damage for your insurer and call the 24/7 line so a replacement can be booked. If the heater only has a failed part, water heater repair may cost far less.

Same-day replacement is offered when a plumber and unit are available. Tell the dispatcher it is an after-hours call and whether the tank is still flooding, so the visit is planned around it.

How a Replacement Visit Goes

Most replacements finish in a few hours when the swap is like-for-like.

  1. 1Confirm the match.The plumber checks the old tank's size, fuel, venting and connections, then confirms the replacement.
  2. 2Drain and disconnect.The old heater is drained, disconnected and carried out.
  3. 3Set the new unit.The new heater is placed, with a drain pan and a proper discharge pipe for the relief valve where required.
  4. 4Connect and vent.Water, gas or electric and venting are reconnected, and an expansion tank is added if your system needs one.
  5. 5Test and walk through.The plumber tests for leaks, sets the temperature, and shows you the shutoff and the relief valve.

If you are upgrading rather than reacting, the planned route is described under water heater installation.

Choosing the Replacement Type

The replacement does not have to match the failed unit, but each option has trade-offs.

TypeStrengthWatch for
Standard gas tankFast recovery, familiar ventingNeeds a sound flue and gas line
Standard electric tankSimple, no ventingNeeds adequate electrical capacity
Heat pump (hybrid)Lower running costNeeds space, airflow and a condensate drain
TanklessEndless hot water on demandHigher upfront cost, venting and gas sizing, see tankless water heater

Sizing and Older Shrewsbury Homes

As a rule of thumb, a 40 gallon tank suits one or two people, 50 gallons suits a family of three or four, and a larger household may need 75 gallons or more. Bigger tanks need more space and more fuel, so sizing is a conversation, not a guess. Older Shrewsbury houses can have cramped utility areas that limit what fits.

Older houses may also have a cramped utility area, old venting or galvanized supply pipes that corrode at the heater connection, and plumbers cover Westborough houses of the same kind. We check those on the visit, since a new tank on a failing connection is a short-lived fix.

Permits and Disposal

Replacing a water heater is normally permitted work in Massachusetts, so the plumber pulls the permit and the inspector checks the installation. If you have questions about permit status, ask the Shrewsbury building department. The old tank is removed and recycled as part of the job.

A heat pump or high-efficiency model may qualify for incentives, and those change often. Ask your utility what is available before you buy.

What to Do When the Tank Starts Leaking

Close the cold-water valve above the tank and turn off the power or the gas. Do not touch the unit while standing in water. Move anything stored near it and call the 24/7 line.

A replacement tank must meet the state plumbing standards in 248 CMR 10.00, including a proper relief valve and discharge.
