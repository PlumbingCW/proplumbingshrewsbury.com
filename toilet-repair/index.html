Overflowing Toilet: Stop the Water First

Lift the tank lid and push the flapper down to stop water entering the bowl, then close the small valve behind the toilet by turning it clockwise. Do not flush again. Mop up with towels, and if water has reached the floor, call the 24/7 line.

If the toilet overflows again after being plunged, the clog may be in the drain, not the toilet. Other fixtures backing up point to a main line problem, and sewer line cleaning is the next step.

What the Toilet Is Telling You

Most toilet repairs come down to a few inexpensive parts, and the key is finding the right one.

A Leak at the Base Is a Floor Problem

A toilet that weeps at the base is leaking through the wax ring, and the water soaks the subfloor over time. The wax ring seals the toilet to the flange that connects to the drain. If the flange is cracked or sits too low, a new ring alone will not hold, and the flange needs a repair.

Act on this one soon, because a slow leak can rot the subfloor before you see it on the ceiling below. If the ceiling or wall below is already stained, leak detection can confirm where the water is going.

Replacing or Installing a Toilet

Replacing a toilet is a good idea when the porcelain is cracked, the toilet clogs repeatedly or the fixture is very old. A plumber removes the old toilet, checks the flange, sets a new wax ring and tests the seal and flush. We also install toilets in new bathrooms and during remodels.

Measure the distance from the wall to the center of the drain flange before shopping, because most toilets are made to fit a standard rough-in. Tell the dispatcher what you plan to buy and we will confirm it fits.

New Bathrooms and Remodels

A toilet in a new or remodeled bathroom needs a drain, a vent and a supply, and the rough-in must be right before the walls close. That belongs to plumbing installation, with the finish toilet set at the end. We work on bathroom projects in Shrewsbury and in Grafton, where many older houses get their first second bathroom.

When Plunging Is Not Enough

A plunger works on a clog right at the toilet. If water drains very slowly, gurgles in other fixtures or the toilet clogs again within a day, the problem is deeper and needs a cable. Chemical drain openers do not help a toilet and can burn skin when the trap is opened.

What to Do Before Pulling a Toilet

Shut off the supply valve, flush to empty the tank and keep the area dry. Do not lift a toilet alone, because they are heavy and the porcelain chips.

A new wax ring and flange repair follow the state plumbing standards in 248 CMR 10.00.
