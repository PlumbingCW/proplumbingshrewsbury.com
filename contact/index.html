What to Tell the Dispatcher

Give the street address and the town first. Then say what is happening, such as water on the floor, a backed-up drain or no hot water, and whether you have already shut anything off.

If you know the age of the house or the type of water heater, mention that too. It helps the plumber bring the right parts.
