Cleaning Drains That Smell in Shrewsbury, MA

By the Shrewsbury Pro Plumbing teamShrewsbury, MA homeowner guide

A drain that smells is trying to tell you something. This guide covers the common causes, the cleaning steps that work and the smells that mean it is time to call.

Call (508) 389-4045

Find Out Where the Smell Comes From

A smell from one drain points to that fixture's trap or branch. A smell from several fixtures, or from the basement floor drain, points to the main line or the vent system. Knowing which one you have saves time.

Run the water for a minute and sniff at the opening. If the smell fades, it was likely a dry trap. If it stays strong, buildup in the pipe is the likelier cause.

Common Smells and What They Suggest

A gas smell is not a drain issue and is treated as an emergency.

Cleaning a Smelly Drain at Home

These steps remove the biofilm that most drain odors come from.

  1. 1Remove the stopper or strainerPull it out and wipe away the slime that collects underneath.
  2. 2Scrub what you can reachUse a brush on the drain opening and the inside of the pipe a few inches down.
  3. 3Flush with hot waterRun it for a minute. Avoid boiling water on old PVC joints or porcelain.
  4. 4Clean the trap if it is accessiblePlace a bucket under the P-trap, unscrew it and rinse it out.
  5. 5Refill unused trapsRun water in guest bathrooms and basement drains so the trap seal stays full.

Dry Traps, Vents and Hidden Causes

Every drain has a curved trap that holds a plug of water to block sewer gas. If a fixture is rarely used, that water evaporates and the smell comes through. A seldom-used basement floor drain is the classic example.

A blocked plumbing vent can also pull water out of traps as fixtures drain, which causes gurgling and odor. A roof vent clogged by leaves or a bird nest is a plumber's job to clear.

Skip the Chemical Fix

Caustic cleaners can sit in a blocked pipe and make the next repair more hazardous. A snake, a brush and hot water are safer, and a jetter handles what they cannot.

When Cleaning Is Not Enough

If the smell returns within days, the buildup is deeper in the line than you can reach. Hydro jetting scours the pipe wall with high-pressure water, which removes the film that holds the odor. Our drain cleaning page explains the snake and basic options.

A sewage smell with a gurgling drain or water backing up is a stop-and-call moment. Stop using water and call the 24/7 line.

The Overflow Opening and Other Hidden Spots

Bathroom sinks have a hidden overflow channel behind the front of the basin. Soap and moisture build a film there, and it can smell sour or musty. Pour a cup of hot water mixed with a little dish soap down the opening, and scrub with a bottle brush if you can reach it.

Garbage disposals also hold odors on the underside of the splash guard and in the grinding chamber. A scrub with a brush under the rubber guard removes most of the film.

Septic Homes Are Different

A house on septic has its own odor patterns. A smell near the tank lid, soggy ground over the field or slow drains in every fixture all suggest the septic system needs attention. Chemical cleaners can harm the bacteria that treat the waste.

We clear the house drain lines, and a septic professional handles the tank and field. Tell the dispatcher which system you have so the right person comes.

What to Do When the Smell Is Strong

Open a window, run the exhaust fan and keep everyone out of the room if the smell is strong or makes you dizzy. Do not mix cleaners, because bleach with ammonia or acid cleaners releases harmful gas. Do call a plumber if the smell lasts after you refill the traps.

Plumbing vents and traps are set by the state plumbing standards in 248 CMR 10.00, which is why a plumber checks them.

Odors in Older Shrewsbury Homes

Many Shrewsbury houses date from the streetcar suburb years and still have original basement floor drains and old drain lines. Those drains are the ones most likely to run dry in winter or collect debris in a pit.

If a smell appears after heavy rain, ask Shrewsbury's public works or sewer office whether street work or a main issue is affecting your area. They can answer for your street.
