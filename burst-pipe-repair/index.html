Burst Pipe: Do These Things First

Close the main water shutoff, usually where the line enters the basement near the meter, and open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the pipes. If water is near outlets or the electrical panel, leave the area and cut power only from a dry spot. Then call the 24/7 line, because the repair cannot start until the leak is isolated.

Move belongings out of the way, soak up standing water and photograph everything before cleaning, since your insurer will want to see it. If a pipe is frozen but not yet burst, keep the heat on and open the faucet slightly, and call us before using a torch or heat gun.

Why Pipes Burst

A burst is the end of a slow failure, and the cause decides whether a single repair is enough.

  • Freezing: ice expands inside a pipe and splits it, usually near a wall, a garage or an unheated crawlspace.
  • Corrosion: old galvanized or copper pipes thin from the inside until a weak spot gives way.
  • High water pressure: pressure above the normal range strains fittings and fixtures.
  • Failed joints and fittings: a poor solder joint or a cracked plastic fitting can blow out.
  • Impact or settling: a nail through a pipe or a shifted wall can break a line.

How a Burst Pipe Repair Works

A typical repair follows five steps.

  1. 1Isolate and drain.The plumber confirms the main is off and drains the line.
  2. 2Open the wall only where needed.Moisture checks show how far the damage runs, and the opening is kept small.
  3. 3Cut out the damaged section.The split pipe is removed back to sound material.
  4. 4Install new pipe.New copper, PEX or other approved pipe is joined with proper fittings.
  5. 5Test and restore water.The line is pressure tested and the water is turned back on slowly.

If the pipe was not the only problem, leak detection can check the rest of the system, and old corroded runs may point to repiping.

Frozen Pipes and Thawing Safely

A frozen pipe has not burst yet. Open the nearest faucet, apply gentle heat with a hair dryer starting near the faucet and working back, and never use an open flame. If you cannot find the frozen section or water is already leaking, call the 24/7 line, and if the boiler has stopped, see boiler repair.

What to Do While You Wait for the Plumber

Do not use a space heater or any electrical device near standing water. Do not try to thaw a frozen pipe with an open flame. Do move valuables, open the cabinet doors under affected sinks and keep the line to the dispatcher open.

Plumbing repairs in Massachusetts follow the state plumbing standards in 248 CMR 10.00, the Uniform State Plumbing Code, and a plumber works to that code when replacing a split section.
