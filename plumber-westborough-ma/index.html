Westborough at a Glance

Westborough is a town of older village streets and busy commercial strips, and the plumbing calls follow both.

  • Town: incorporated in 1717, with about 21,600 residents counted in the 2020 census.
  • Main roads: Route 9 runs through town, the Massachusetts Turnpike and I-495 meet nearby, and Routes 30 and 135 join near the center.
  • Water: town-run wells and the Westboro Reservoir, managed by the Westboro Water Department.
  • Emergency dispatch: available around the clock for homes and businesses, with a plumber sent to the address.
  • ZIP code: 01581 covers the town.

Route 9, the Interchange and the Center: Where Calls Come From

The commercial strip along Route 9 and the offices near the turnpike and I-495 interchange keep commercial plumbing busy, from restaurant grease lines to restroom leaks. A backed-up kitchen drain on a Friday evening is the kind of call that needs hydro jetting and a plumber who can come after hours.

Older houses cluster around the center, where Routes 30 and 135 meet, and in the Vintonville and West Main Street historic districts. Houses of that age often still have cast iron drains and galvanized supply lines in the basement. If your pressure has been fading or a drain keeps slowing, those pipes are the first place to look.

Wells, a Reservoir and Your Water

The Westboro Water Department draws from several town wells, including sources on Hopkinton Road, Morse Street and Otis Street, plus the Westboro Reservoir. Groundwater tends to carry dissolved minerals, and minerals build up inside water heaters over time. The department does not publish a single hardness number we can quote, so ask it for its latest water quality report or test your tap before sizing a heater.

For a failing tank, water heater replacement is the usual answer once the shell leaks, and a sediment flush on a younger unit can buy time.
