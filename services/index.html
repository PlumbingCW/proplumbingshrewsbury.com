Pick the Service You Need

Every page below explains what the work involves, what affects the price, how to prepare and what to expect when a plumber arrives. 15 of them are emergency services, and the 24/7 line answers for those at any hour.

If you are not sure which page fits, call the line and describe what you see. A dispatcher can point you to the right visit.

Drain Cleaning

Snaking and cabling for clogged sinks, tubs, floor drains and main lines.

Water Heater Repair

Leaks, no hot water, pilot and thermostat faults on tank units.

Water Heater Replacement

Failed tank out, new unit in, usually the same day.

Water Heater Installation

Planned installs, upgrades and fuel switches, sized to the household.

Tankless Water Heater

Install, repair and flush service for gas and electric tankless units.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure water to scour grease, scale and roots from drain lines.

Leak Detection

Finding hidden leaks, running meters, wet ceilings and high water bills.

Sewer Line Repair

Spot repair, trenchless lining and replacement for failing sewer laterals.

Sewer Camera Inspection

A video look inside the line before you repair, buy or dig.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Clearing main sewer backups and root-choked laterals.

Commercial Plumbing

Service for shops, restaurants, offices and multi-unit buildings.

Toilet Repair & Installation

Running, leaking, clogged and rocking toilets, plus new installs.

Faucet Repair

Drips, dead cartridges, failed shutoff valves and sink fixtures.

Garbage Disposal

Jammed, leaking or dead disposals repaired or replaced.

Repiping

Whole-house or partial pipe replacement for failing galvanized and old copper.

Plumbing Installation

New fixtures, new bathrooms and remodel rough-in work.

Burst Pipe Repair

Shutoff, cut-out and repair of split or frozen pipes.

Water Service Line Repair

Repair and replacement of the line from the street to your house.

Sump Pump Repair

Failed pumps, stuck floats and new sump pump installs.

Basement Waterproofing

Interior drainage, sump systems and wet-basement diagnosis.

Boiler Repair

No-heat calls, leaks, gas valves and circulator problems on hot water boilers.
