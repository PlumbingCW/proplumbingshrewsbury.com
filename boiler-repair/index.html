No Heat: What to Do While You Wait

A boiler that has stopped in winter can lead to frozen pipes if the house keeps cooling. Check that the thermostat is calling for heat, the boiler switch is on and the pressure gauge reads in its normal range. If you smell gas or a carbon monoxide alarm sounds, leave the house and call 911 or the gas utility, not us.

Why Boiler Calls Are Gas Valve and Circulator Calls

Gas boilers need a gas valve that opens only when the safety controls agree. When the valve fails or sticks, the boiler locks out, and it takes a licensed technician to test and replace it safely. Circulator pumps run for years, then fail in cold weather when they work hardest.

Both are repairs, not replacements, in most cases. The plumber diagnoses, tells you the cost and replaces the part, often in the same visit.

New England Boiler Systems

Many New England homes heat with a boiler feeding baseboard or radiators, not forced air. Older Shrewsbury houses may have cast iron radiators and original piping that needs more care than a modern system. Tell the dispatcher the fuel, because oil and gas systems are repaired by different technicians.

A cold boiler also puts pipes in outside walls and garages at risk. If water is already escaping, burst pipe repair is the next call, and for a failed boiler past repair we help you weigh replacement options.

Keeping a Boiler Running Through the Cold Months

A few checks before the first cold snap prevent many winter no-heat calls.

  • Check the pressure gauge: a reading well below normal means water is leaking out somewhere.
  • Bleed radiators that stay cold at the top: trapped air blocks hot water from filling them.
  • Keep the area around the boiler clear: storage near a burner can block combustion air.
  • Listen for new sounds: banging, whining or clicking often shows up days before a failure.
  • Have a technician service it each year: cleaning and testing safety controls prevents lockouts.

If you are adding a zone or moving baseboard, that falls under plumbing installation, and the same service covers Westborough and Grafton boilers.

What to Do If You Smell Gas or See Soot

Do not light anything, flip switches or use a phone inside if you smell gas. Leave the house, then call 911 and the gas utility from outside. Do not restart a boiler that is sooting until it has been inspected.
