Edgemere at a Glance

The facts below come from town planning material and from real estate sites, and the list says which is which.

  • Name: used for a Shrewsbury neighborhood by Redfin, homes.com and NeighborhoodScout. Those are real estate sites, so treat the name as common usage and not as an official designation.
  • Anchor street: Edgemere Boulevard, which appears in the study area of the town's Route 20 corridor planning material along with Lakeside Drive and Lake Street.
  • Former drive-in: the same document describes the former Edgemere Drive-In parcel, zoned Rural B and proposed for rezoning to Commercial Business, with a mixed-use project anticipated. We did not check its current status.
  • Boundary: none documented. This page describes the area by its anchor street and does not draw a line.

Residential Plumbing Near a Busy Corridor

Edgemere sits near Route 20, which is also called the Hartford Turnpike, so the area mixes homes with roadside businesses. Most visits to a neighborhood like this are ordinary ones: a slow drain, a water heater that no longer keeps up, a dripping faucet or a toilet that keeps running.

Those jobs have their own pages. See drain cleaning, water heater repair, faucet repair and toilet repair for what each visit involves and how to prepare. Plumbing work in Massachusetts falls under the state plumbing standards in 248 CMR 10.00.

Sewer Work on Route 20 and Lake Street

A 2020 town release on the Route 20 corridor sewer upgrades described new sewer mains on Lake Street from Arrowwood Drive to Route 20 and along Route 20 from Lake Street to the Worcester city line. It also covered upgrades to pump stations on Arrowwood Drive and at Route 20 and Lakeside Drive.

That release does not say which houses connect to those mains. If you are unsure whether your street is on town sewer, ask the Shrewsbury DPW Water and Sewer Division. The answer tells you who is responsible for a pipe and what a repair involves.
